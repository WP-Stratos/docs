The WP Stratos Cloud Panel is the control interface for everything you do on the platform. It's accessible at [cloud.wpstratos.com](https://cloud.wpstratos.com), and it gives you a clear view of every site on your account along with the tools to configure domains, protect your data with backups, and safely test changes in staging. None of it requires server expertise, and none of it requires a support ticket for routine work.

If you're running WP Stratos as the backbone of your own small hosting business, the Cloud Panel is worth understanding well beyond the basics. It's a single pane you can use to manage every client site you host, tag and organize them however makes sense for your workflow, and hand a client secure, scoped access to their own site without giving them the keys to your whole account.

## What Else Lives in the Panel

Beyond the sections above, each site's detail page carries a few more tools worth knowing about:

- **One-click WP Admin.** A Log In button that uses single sign-on to drop you straight into a site's wp-admin, no separate WordPress password to remember.
- **Traffic analytics.** Visits, unique visitors, average session time, and bandwidth, charted over the last week, month, or year, so you can see whether a site's traffic pattern actually matches what a client is telling you.
- **Tags and sharing.** Tag sites however fits your workflow (by client, by plan, by priority) and share individual sites with teammates or clients using scoped, role-based access, so nobody needs your master login to check on their own site.
- **Hosting-level tools.** Every account also has an underlying hosting area with FTP/SFTP accounts, MySQL database access and phpMyAdmin, cron job scheduling, a file manager, and the DNS zone editor covered in [DNS Configuration](/domains/dns-configuration). Most day-to-day work never touches this layer, but it's there when you need direct file or database access.

## Logging In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Cloud Panel">
    Open your browser and navigate to [cloud.wpstratos.com](https://cloud.wpstratos.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type in your account email address and password, then click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
    You arrive at the main dashboard, where all your sites and account activity are displayed at a glance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dashboard Overview

Once you're logged in, the Cloud Panel dashboard is organized into a few main areas.

**Instance list.** The central panel shows every WordPress site on your account, in either a list or tile view. Each entry displays the site name, domain, current status, and quick-action buttons for wp-admin access and settings.

**Notifications.** A bell icon in the top navigation bar surfaces important alerts such as completed backups, SSL status changes, and available WordPress or plugin updates.

**Account settings.** Your profile menu in the top right corner leads to **My Account**, where you manage two-factor authentication, your password, personal info, new device detection notifications, and your list of active logged-in devices. See [Security & Account Settings](/cloud-panel/security-and-account) for the full walkthrough. Billing is handled separately at [billing.wpstratos.com](https://billing.wpstratos.com).
