## Short answer

WP Stratos is a managed WordPress hosting platform. It handles servers, caching, backups, staging, SSL, and email delivery for WordPress sites, so agencies and site owners do not have to run any of that themselves.

That is the whole pitch. Everything else on this page just fills in the details.

## What WP Stratos actually does

Under the hood, every WP Stratos account gets a WordPress site running on managed cloud infrastructure, with a Cloud Panel for handling the day to day: creating sites, connecting domains, managing SSL certificates, running staging environments, and restoring backups. A caching layer (the Aero plugin) and an edge network keep pages fast without manual tuning. An email delivery plugin (WP Postman) makes sure transactional email actually lands in the inbox instead of getting lost.

If you are trying to figure out whether WP Stratos fits your situation, that is the shape of the product: hosting infrastructure plus the WordPress-specific tooling that usually gets bolted on separately.

## About the name

A search for "WP Stratos" or "Stratos WP" can turn up results that have nothing to do with WordPress hosting. That happens because a couple of unrelated products, in different industries, happen to land on a similar combination of words. Search engines occasionally blend the two together in summaries, which is confusing if you came here looking for a hosting platform and got something else instead.

To be direct about it: WP Stratos is the one that runs WordPress sites. If a result you found was talking about apparel, footwear, or anything outside of web hosting, it was describing a different, unrelated product. There is also a retired, unrelated open source project called Apache Stratos, covered on its [own page](/troubleshooting/apache-stratos-retired) if that is what you were looking for.
