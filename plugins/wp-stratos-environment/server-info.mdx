Separate from the welcome banner, WP Stratos Environment adds its own widget to your regular WordPress **Dashboard** screen, alongside the usual "At a Glance" and Activity widgets. It only shows up once WP Postman is installed and active, since there's nothing to report without it.

## Stats row

Four numbers sit across the top: **Total (30d)**, **Sent**, **Delivered**, and **Failed**. They're pulled straight from WP Postman's own mail log table, so what you see here always lines up with what WP Postman itself is tracking.

## Recent activity

Below the stats, up to 15 of your most recent send attempts, each with a color-coded status badge (Sent, Delivered, Failed, or Pending), the subject line trimmed to a few words with the full text on hover, the recipient, and how long ago the message went out. Anything from the last hour reads as "Xm ago"; older mail rolls over to hours, then days, then a plain date.

## Refresh

The **Refresh** button pulls a fresh copy of both the stats and the log without reloading the whole dashboard page. Send a test message and hit refresh to confirm it landed without leaving the screen.

## Going deeper

This widget is a glance, not an investigation tool. For the full picture on any specific message, exact status, error text, and the rendered message body, open the [Mail Log](/plugins/wp-postman/email-logs) inside WP Postman's own settings, reachable directly from the widget's footer links: **Full Mail Log** and **Settings**.

<Tip>
  A run of Failed rows here is your earliest signal that something's wrong with delivery. See [Email Delivery](/troubleshooting/email-delivery) for common causes and how to fix them.
</Tip>
