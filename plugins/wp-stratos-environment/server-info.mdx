WP Stratos Environment adds a small widget to your main WordPress **Dashboard** screen, alongside the usual "At a Glance" and Activity widgets. It shows recent outgoing email activity from WP Postman, the pre-installed SMTP plugin, so you can spot a delivery problem without navigating away from the dashboard.

## What the Widget Shows

The widget surfaces a compact view of your most recent send attempts: recipient, subject, delivery method, and status. It's meant for a quick glance, not deep investigation, if you're actually troubleshooting a specific failed message, the full [Mail Log](/plugins/wp-postman/email-logs) inside WP Postman's own settings has the complete detail: exact error text, HTTP response codes, and the full message body.

<Tip>
  Think of this widget as an early-warning signal. If you land on your dashboard and see a run of Failed rows, that's your cue to open WP Postman's Mail Log and dig into what's actually going wrong. See [Email Delivery](/troubleshooting/email-delivery) for common causes and fixes.
</Tip>

## Where to Find It

Log in to your WordPress admin and go to the main **Dashboard** (the screen you land on after logging in, or reachable from **Dashboard → Home** in the sidebar). The widget appears among your other dashboard widgets. If you don't see it, check that WP Stratos Environment hasn't been deactivated, see [Plugin Reactivation](/troubleshooting/plugin-reactivation) if it keeps disappearing.

<Note>
  This widget is display-only. You can't take action on a message from here, restore, resend, and detailed diagnostics all happen from WP Postman's own [Mail Log](/plugins/wp-postman/email-logs) screen.
</Note>
