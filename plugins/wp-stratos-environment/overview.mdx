WP Stratos Environment ships pre-installed and pre-activated on every WP Stratos site. It's the plugin doing the quiet, structural work behind the scenes: marking your site as WP Stratos-hosted, pulling live status from Aero and WP Postman into one dashboard view, and handling the install for both of those plugins when the initial auto-install fails. New plugin releases will install & upgrade all managed plugins regardless of WP Stratos Environment.

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Platform Headers" icon="server">
    Adds identifying HTTP response headers to every frontend, admin, and REST request, so WP Stratos infrastructure, and support, can recognize your site as WP Stratos-hosted the moment they look at it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Welcome Banner" icon="house" href="/plugins/wp-stratos-environment/php-settings">
    A live dashboard banner covering Aero's caching and optimization state, WP Postman's mail delivery health, and one-click actions for both, right where you land after logging in.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One-Click Plugin Install" icon="cloud-arrow-down" href="/plugins/wp-stratos-environment/plugin-installer">
    Downloads and installs Aero and WP Postman straight from their GitHub releases, with a live progress bar.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mail Log Widget" icon="envelope" href="/plugins/wp-stratos-environment/server-info">
    A WP Postman mail log widget on your main Dashboard screen with 30-day send stats and your most recent outgoing messages.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Identifying your site

Every response, whether it's a page load, an admin screen, or a REST API call, carries two headers: `Platform: WP Stratos` and `X-Powered-By: WP Stratos Managed Cloud`. That's how WP Stratos support and internal tooling confirm a site belongs to the platform without asking you first. Open your browser's developer console while inside wp-admin for a little easter egg lol. Don't worry, it's only visible in `/wp-admin` pages and doesn't add any load time.

<Note>
  WP Stratos Environment is pre-installed on every site and reactivates automatically if it's ever switched off, since the headers, dashboard, and [Aero Caching](/plugins/aero/overview) depend on it running. See [Plugin Reactivation](/troubleshooting/plugin-reactivation) if that catches you off guard.
</Note>

## Accessing WP Stratos Environment

There's no separate settings screen to hunt for. Log in to wp-admin and the welcome banner is already on your Dashboard. The mail log widget sits below it, among your other dashboard widgets.
