WP Stratos Environment is a lightweight plugin pre-installed and activated on every WP Stratos site. It doesn't manage PHP settings or expose server configuration, that's handled entirely from the Cloud Panel's [Developer Settings](/cloud-panel/developer-settings) instead. What this plugin actually does is smaller and more focused: it identifies your site as running on the WP Stratos platform, gives you a quick-glance overview of your managed plugins right in wp-admin, and surfaces your recent email activity without needing to leave the dashboard.

## What It Actually Does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Platform Headers" icon="server">
    Adds identifying HTTP response headers to every request, so WP Stratos infrastructure (and support, when you reach out) can recognize your site as WP Stratos-hosted.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Welcome Banner" icon="house" href="/plugins/wp-stratos-environment/php-settings">
    A dashboard banner in wp-admin giving you a quick overview of the three managed plugins, WP Postman, Aero, and WP Stratos Environment itself, plus a handful of at-a-glance settings.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mail Log Widget" icon="envelope" href="/plugins/wp-stratos-environment/server-info">
    Adds a WP Postman mail log widget to your wp-admin dashboard, so you can glance at recent outgoing email activity without opening WP Postman's own settings screen.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  WP Stratos Environment is pre-installed on all WP Stratos sites. If you deactivate it, it will be automatically reactivated on the next plugin update. See [Plugin Reactivation](/troubleshooting/plugin-reactivation) if that's unexpected.
</Note>

## What It Doesn't Do

Worth being explicit about, since it's a common point of confusion: this plugin does not control your PHP version, memory limits, execution time, or upload size. PHP version is changed from the Cloud Panel's [Developer Settings](/cloud-panel/developer-settings), per domain. Other PHP configuration values (memory limits, upload sizes, and so on) aren't self-service anywhere, plugin or Cloud Panel, and require reaching out to WP Stratos support at [missioncontrol@wpstratos.com](mailto:missioncontrol@wpstratos.com).

## Accessing WP Stratos Environment

Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard and select **WP Stratos Environment** from the left sidebar to see the welcome banner and managed plugin overview. The mail log widget appears automatically on your main **Dashboard** screen alongside your other WordPress widgets.
