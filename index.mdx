## Get up and running

New to WP Stratos? Follow these four steps to go from sign-up to a fully configured WordPress site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at WP Stratos and verify your email address to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch your site">
    Open the Cloud Panel and click **Add New**, then **Create New WordPress Instance**. Pick a theme, choose which hosting service to build on if you have more than one, and give your site a name. WP Stratos provisions the install in the background, usually within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your domain">
    Open your site and click **Change Domain**. Choose **Set DNS Records** to point your domain at WP Stratos without touching your nameservers, or **Set Nameservers** to hand DNS over to a WP Stratos-managed Cloudflare pair generated for that domain. SSL is provisioned automatically once your domain resolves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your plugins">
    Add your API key or SMTP credentials in WP Postman so outbound email starts flowing, and confirm caching is active in Aero (it's on by default, so most sites don't need to touch it). WP Stratos Environment doesn't need any setup, it just gives you a quick overview of the other two.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Migrate an existing site, with zero downtime

Already running WordPress somewhere else? You don't have to take the site offline to move it. WP Stratos builds your migrated site as a new, separate instance, so the original keeps serving visitors the entire time you're moving it over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Point WP Stratos at your current site">
    In the Cloud Panel, click **Add New**, then **Import Existing WordPress Instance**, and enter your current site's URL. If the domain isn't resolving yet, expand **Advanced** and enter the server IP directly instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a data center">
    Choose Ashburn (VA), Los Angeles (CA), Amsterdam (NL), or Dallas (TX), whichever sits closest to the bulk of your visitors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your transfer method">
    The migration plugin handles the overwhelming majority of sites automatically. If your current host won't allow it, provide FTP/SFTP details instead and WP Stratos pulls the files over directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the copy, then cut over on your own schedule">
    Once the migrated site checks out, follow the [Connect a Domain](/domains/connect-domain) guide to point your domain at the new instance. Nothing about your original site changes until you make that switch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A migration never touches your original site. It stays online, exactly as it is, until you decide to repoint the domain, so there's no downtime window to plan around.
</Note>
